              How PCA uses SVD▾
            

            

              

                Centre the data into X (n×3), then take its SVD:
                X = U Σ Vᵀ. The covariance becomes

                  C = XᵀX/(n−1) = V (Σ²/(n−1)) Vᵀ

                — already diagonalised. So the PCs are V's columns, the
                variances are λᵢ = σᵢ²/(n−1), and each flatten step
                Pk = VkVkᵀ just keeps V's
                first k columns.
